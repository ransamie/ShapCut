Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about running ShapCut locally.

Is ShapCut really 100% free forever?

Yes. ShapCut is fully open-source software distributed under MIT and LGPL licenses. There are no subscriptions, no monthly credits, no paywalled features, and no watermark on exported videos.

Does my video footage ever leave my computer?

Never. Unlike SaaS video clippers that upload your files to remote third-party cloud servers, ShapCut runs the Whisper AI neural network and FFmpeg media processor 100% locally on your own machine.

Do I need an expensive graphics card?

No. ShapCut uses optimized CTranslate2 INT8 quantization, allowing fast transcription on standard multi-core CPUs. If an NVIDIA GPU or Apple Silicon Neural Engine is present, ShapCut automatically utilizes it for lightning-speed rendering.

What video formats are supported?

ShapCut supports any video format handled by FFmpeg, including MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WEBM, and M4V at resolutions from 720p up to 4K 60FPS.
