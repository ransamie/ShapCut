Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about running ShapCut locally on your computer.

Is ShapCut really 100% free forever?

Yes. ShapCut is fully open-source software distributed under MIT and LGPL licenses. There are no paid tiers, no monthly credits, no paywalled features, and absolutely no watermark stamped on exported clips.

Does my video footage ever leave my computer?

Never. Unlike SaaS web clippers that upload your files to remote third-party cloud servers, ShapCut runs Whisper AI and FFmpeg 100% locally on your machine. You can even disconnect your internet entirely and ShapCut works seamlessly.

Do I need an expensive graphics card?

No. ShapCut uses optimized CTranslate2 INT8 quantization, enabling fast transcription on standard multi-core CPUs. If an NVIDIA GPU (CUDA) or Apple Silicon Neural Engine is present, ShapCut automatically detects and leverages it for lightning-fast processing.

What video formats and resolutions are supported?

ShapCut supports any video or audio container handled by FFmpeg, including MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WEBM, and M4V at resolutions from 720p up to full 4K 60FPS.

How does ShapCut differ from Opus Clip or Descript?

Cloud tools charge $240–$580/year, impose monthly minute quotas, add watermarks, and own your footage on their servers. ShapCut is a native desktop app that gives you the exact same AI clipping capabilities for $0/year, with complete privacy and zero limits.
